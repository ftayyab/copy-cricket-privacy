COPY CRICKET

Privacy Policy

Effective date: 8 October 2026
Android app: Copy Cricket · com.copycricket.game

Copy Cricket plays offline. The game does not collect or transmit personal information, and saved matches stay on your device.

About this policy

This policy explains how the Copy Cricket Android game handles information. Privacy inquiries can be sent to the Copy Cricket developer at Ftayyab82@gmail.com. It covers the current offline release, version 1.3.1.

Information handled by the game

The game does not require an account and does not collect your name, email address, location, contacts, photos, advertising identifier or other personal information. It does not transmit gameplay data to the developer or a server.

Match progress, selected teams and match statistics are stored locally so you can resume playing. Player names inside the game are generated game content, not information about you.

Advertising, analytics and sharing

The game contains no advertising or analytics SDKs, tracking cookies, cloud synchronization or in-app purchases. We do not sell or share information collected through gameplay because the game does not collect it.

Storage, security and deletion

Saved matches remain in the app's local storage until overwritten or removed. The app does not enable Android backup for its app data. Local information is protected by Android's app storage controls; you should also protect your device with a screen lock.

To delete saved game data, open Android Settings, select Apps → Copy Cricket → Storage, and choose Clear storage or Clear data. Uninstalling the app also removes its local saved data. There is no online account to delete.

If you contact us

If you email the developer, your email address and the information you choose to send are processed to respond to your inquiry. These messages are handled by the email provider, Gmail, and are separate from gameplay. Please do not send unnecessary sensitive information. You can request deletion of your correspondence by emailing the same address; records may be retained where needed to resolve the inquiry or meet applicable legal obligations.

Children's privacy

The game does not collect personal information from players of any age. Children should ask a parent or guardian for help before sending a support email.

This website and platform services

This policy page has no forms, analytics scripts or embedded third-party media. The hosting provider may process ordinary technical request information, such as IP addresses, to deliver and secure the webpage. Google Play and Android may separately process information under their own privacy policies; this is separate from data collection by the game.

Changes to this policy

We will update this page and its effective date if the game's data practices change. Any future addition of online services, advertising or analytics will be reflected in an updated policy and the relevant Google Play disclosures.

Contact

For privacy questions or requests, email Ftayyab82@gmail.com.
